anim: played cards fly from hand to pile with a brief flip-in; the pile badge bumps with animate-[tick_.25s] each time a card lands. Doubt ring is urgent (red, ticking) but the button itself never shakes — panic without chaos. Toasts stack bottom-up, oldest expires first, each animate-slide-up in, fade+collapse out.
